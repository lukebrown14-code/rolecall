# Fit — Lyrebird Media, Senior Platform Engineer

**Marginal.** No blockers, but the core of the role is a Kubernetes migration, and the Profile
has no production Kubernetes, Helm or Argo CD.

## Must-haves

- Five or more years in platform engineering: partly met (six years, about four on platform work).
- Production Kubernetes, Helm, Argo CD: not met.
- Terraform and AWS: met.
- Prometheus and Grafana: met.

## Gaps

- Kubernetes — no production experience, and it is the main deliverable.
- Helm — not in the Profile.
- Argo CD — not in the Profile.
- Mentoring — on-call lead only; no formal mentoring role.

## What would have to be true

The client would need to value the AWS and Terraform migration story enough to accept learning
EKS on the job. Worth a question to the recruiter before writing anything.
