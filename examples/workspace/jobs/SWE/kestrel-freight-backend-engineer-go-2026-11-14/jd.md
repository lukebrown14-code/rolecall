# Backend Engineer (Go) — Kestrel Freight

Melbourne (hybrid, two days in the Docklands office) · Permanent, full time

Kestrel Freight moves refrigerated goods for growers and supermarkets across the eastern
states. Our booking and dispatch platform is a set of Go services on AWS, and we are hiring a
backend engineer to help us split the dispatch monolith into services the team can own.

## What you will do

- Design and build Go services and REST APIs for booking, dispatch and driver apps.
- Own your services in production, including on-call one week in five.
- Work with the platform team on Terraform modules and the GitHub Actions pipeline.
- Improve how we test and observe services, with Prometheus and Grafana.

## What we are looking for

- Three or more years building backend services, ideally in Go.
- Solid SQL, preferably PostgreSQL.
- Experience running services on AWS with Docker.
- Comfortable on an on-call rotation.

## Nice to have

- Event-driven systems (Kafka or SQS).
- Kubernetes.

$150,000 – $170,000 plus super.
