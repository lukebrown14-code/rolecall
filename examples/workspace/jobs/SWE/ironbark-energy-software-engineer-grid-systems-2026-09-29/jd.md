# Software Engineer, Grid Systems — Ironbark Energy

Melbourne (hybrid) · Permanent · $160,000 – $175,000 plus super

Ironbark Energy runs a network of community batteries. The Grid Systems team builds the Go
services that read battery telemetry and decide when to charge and discharge.

## Responsibilities

- Build Go services that process telemetry from 900 batteries in near real time.
- Store and query time-series data in PostgreSQL.
- Keep services observable and on call.

## Requirements

- Go in production.
- PostgreSQL.
- Docker and AWS.
- Prometheus and Grafana.
