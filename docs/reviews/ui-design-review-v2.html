Three regressions from the last round. The fixes shipped in #130/#131/#132 fixed their target but left three new seams. These are the highest priority because they are self-inflicted and cheap to reverse:
  1. Offer celebration is a triple echo — the message now appears in the body banner and the toast and the key line, floating in 24 blank rows on top of an empty [Applied 0] tab.
  2. "u undo" prints twice — the two-line status area keeps the toast's u undo hint and the key line's u undo.
  3. Fit stopped scanning as a column — per-row column widths (7b) let each row drop the slots it doesn't use, so fit lands at a different x on every row.

Small improvements (quick wins)

  • 15-stats: capitalise closed → Closed; the lone █ bar at 60 columns erases 2-vs-7 — always keep a bar's numeric count.
  • 03-applied-all: the Closed · 4 group header starts two columns right of every other header — align it (no glyph, one padding space).
  • 01-leads footer drops : commands while other screens keep it — pin one footer key set across all screens.
  • Settings Provenance column has no header, repeats the file on every row, and says …/001 fixed this session — label the column Source and de-duplicate.
  • 43-settings-tracks-rows: J/K reorder (row pane) vs j k move (group action) live in different footer zones; at 80 only J/K survives. Give each a distinct word and keep both when they fit.
  • Timeline: past and future stops are distinguished only by dim colour; in ASCII/no-colour (32/33) future stages read as already reached — add a → or "next" label.
  • 27-settings-machine-edit: "Edit Editor" reads like a typo — "Edit · Editor" or "Set Editor".
  • 42-add-form-discard: the confirm inherits * required and redraws the whole form — drop the required marker, and show what is lost ("Title: Engineer").
